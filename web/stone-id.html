Stone Material Identifier

Non-invasive visual field guide · Prototype 0.1

Examine the visible surface

Observe the marker without touching, scratching, applying liquids, or disturbing it. One photograph rarely proves rock identity. Choose “Not sure” when a feature is difficult to see.

Candidate materials

Visual comparison only. Results are hypotheses, not definitive geological identification or cleaning advice.

For confirmation, compare museum or geological reference specimens and seek a qualified conservator/geologist if identification affects treatment. Avoid acid, scratch and other tests on historic monuments.

Materials in the reference library

Slate · sandstone / brownstone · marble · limestone · granite · schist · gneiss · soapstone · cast zinc / “white bronze” · concrete / cast stone.

Reference standards: National Park Service, Association for Gravestone Studies, Massachusetts historic burial-ground guidelines.
