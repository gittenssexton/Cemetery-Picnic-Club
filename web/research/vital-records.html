Different state and collection rules apply. For historical sources, preserve each record's original text, page number and source abbreviation. In the Warren book, G.S.2 identifies entries copied from Pine Grove gravestones; those entries do not independently verify that gravestone.

Connect evidence carefully

A source entry, person, grave marker and burial are distinct records. A name/date resemblance creates a research lead, not a confirmed match. Review the source page and original inscription before making any link.
