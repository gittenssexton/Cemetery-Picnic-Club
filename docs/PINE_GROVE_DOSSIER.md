# Pine Grove Cemetery, Warren, Massachusetts — Pilot Dossier v0.1
Prepared November 8, 2026. Sources are linked; treat findings as leads until verified in the field.

## Identity
- Site: Pine Grove Cemetery (also called Pinegrove Cemetery), Warren, Worcester County, Massachusetts.
- Town-reported address: **207 Maple Street, Warren, MA**.
- Approximate cemetery centroid (OpenStreetMap/Mapcarta): **42.20909, -72.19660**. THIS IS NOT A GRAVE LOCATION and must not be presented as one.
- Municipality: Town of Warren; Cemetery Commission and Cemetery Department.
- Rules effective 2026-03-23: visiting **dawn to dusk**. No trespassing after hours. Group lunches or organized events are NOT explicitly authorized by these cemetery rules: ask the Commission before arranging them.

## Historical and operational findings
- Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation's 2008 Warren Reconnaissance Report says the earliest inscription dates to 1746, the cemetery grew from a one-acre beginning, and the older portion lies near Maple Street.
- The same report documents historic eighteenth- and nineteenth-century symbols and a 1876 receiving tomb built by Issachar Comins.
- The town's 2024 annual cemetery report places the facility at 207 Maple Street, identifies town maintenance and cemetery preservation work, and says a mapping project was continuing in the Founders section.
- The 2008 report suggests approximately 15 acres, while the town's 2024 report describes 25 acres; this discrepancy should be checked against current municipal parcel and boundary records before adopting a definitive acreage figure.
- The 2024 report explicitly welcomed adults and students to help with Founders section mapping. This is historical evidence of an opportunity, not proof that the request remains open in November 2026.

## iPhone 16 Pro field protocol
- Use the built-in Camera app first and keep originals in Photos; then attach to Field Notebook. For most images, ordinary high-resolution settings suffice. Use 48 MP or macro selectively for difficult inscriptions; preserve originals and EXIF with a consent-aware policy.
- For each marker: straight-on whole front, close-up of text, artistic feature close-up, rear/edge where accessible, wider context image showing relative position.
- Optional side-light (natural oblique sunlight) can reveal shallow incisions; never apply chalk, wax, pigments, chemicals, pressure, or rubbing.
- Capture approximate GPS with accuracy radius, optional manually corrected map location. Trees can degrade precision.
- iPhone 16 Pro LiDAR is available for a later native depth feature, but the current PWA collects photographs/GPS only.
- Work offline as needed. Export both record JSON and photograph; browser storage alone is not durable archival backup.
