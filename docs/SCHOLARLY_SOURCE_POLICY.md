# Scholarly Sources and Evidence Policy — Cemetery Picnic Club

## Scope
Academic journals, monographs, books, scholarly articles, local histories, cemetery ledgers, gravestone inscriptions, geological surveys, archaeology reports, newspapers and archival materials may all inform the historical database.

**Citation is necessary for traceability but is not a copyright license.** Facts and ideas can normally be summarized in original wording, while copyrighted photographs, illustrations, maps, extended quotations, and substantial original text or tables need permission, a qualifying license, public-domain status, or another lawful basis for reuse. Do not assume 'academic' means open access or unrestricted reuse.

## Store bibliographic metadata
- source_id; work_type; title; authors/editors; publication_year; publisher or journal; volume, issue, edition; DOI/ISBN/URL; archive/catalog reference; page/figure/plate/folio number; access date; rights/license; provenance notes.
- If original citation information is unavailable, mark as incomplete; do not manufacture it.
- Each extractable claim needs a separate citation pinpoint when possible.

## Claim representation
- Subject: person, marker, inscription, cemetery, carver, workshop, quarry, material, symbol, family relationship, location, conservation practice.
- Predicate: e.g. 'attributed_to', 'made_of', 'located_at', 'related_to', 'depicts', 'dated_to'.
- Value/linked entity, source_id, exact locator, quotation or original paraphrase, confidence, author/reviewer, methodology, time scope, uncertainty, contradiction links.
- Keep distinctions among (a) direct evidence (signature, municipal ledger), (b) published scholarly attribution, (c) the team's inference, and (d) unverified external user contribution.
- Different scholars may disagree; store competing assertions rather than erasing one.

## Images, copyrighted works and AI
- Use original field photographs or images with verified rights; link externally instead of copying protected book plates/photos without authorization.
- Short necessary quotations with citation may sometimes be permissible, but avoid systematic reproduction; public-domain, permission, licensed, and fair-use assessments are distinct and contextual.
- Text/data mining for machine learning and bulk extraction has additional legal, contractual, and platform constraints; do not assume scholarly access grants AI training rights.
- Retain copyright notices and contributor credit when applicable.
